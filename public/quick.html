Amaze locally: eligible purchases can earn 4 miles/$, including supermarket taps. Fee: 1%, minimum S$0.50. Avoid it for small local buys.

Two caps: Citi + Amaze share S$1,000 per statement month. Standard HSBC: S$1,000 per calendar month.

Small buys: UOB and Trust round each purchase down to S$5 blocks. SimplyGo uses accumulated posted charges.

Flights / hotels: Citi’s online 4 miles/$ excludes travel, including through Amaze. Compare separately.

Assumptions & sources

Fixed reference for HSBC Revolution, Citi Rewards Mastercard, KrisFlyer UOB Credit Card and Trust Freedom. It does not read your connected wallet or remaining caps. No sign-in is needed.

Assumes Trust Miles mode, UOB’s S$1,000 annual qualifying spend met, available bonus allowances and HSBC’s standard 10× tier. Rates are before rounding and transfer fees. HSBC uses the KrisFlyer conversion ratio; other airline ratios differ. UOB’s extra category miles arrive after the membership year.

Local defaults avoid added payment-route fees; they do not maximise miles regardless of cost. Amaze + Citi’s 4 miles/$ is a reported pairing, subject to Citi’s exclusions and shared cap. Cash, ordinary PayNow/NETS and excluded bills or top-ups do not become eligible retail.

HSBC’s bonus requires eligible contactless or online payment. Inserting the card can change the result. Merchant names do not prove MCC or card acceptance, and online checkouts can code differently.
