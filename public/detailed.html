Caps, rounding & fees

HSBC Revolution
Standard bonus covers S$1,000 each calendar month. 10 points/$ converts to 3.33 KrisFlyer miles/$; base is 0.33. Eligible spend is aggregated monthly and rounded down to S$1; individual amount results are marginal estimates. Other airline conversion ratios differ.
Citi Rewards / Amaze + Citi
One shared S$1,000 statement-month bonus allowance. Citi rounds each purchase down to S$1. Eligible bonus is 4 miles/$; base is 0.4. Enter an amount to see partial-cap effects. Amaze pairing is based on independent reporting and does not remove merchant exclusions.
KrisFlyer UOB
S$5 blocks per transaction. Eligible category rate is 2.4 miles/$ with S$1,000 qualifying SIA / Scoot / KrisShop spend per membership year; otherwise 1.2. The extra 1.2 category miles/$ arrives after that year. Qualifying SIA-group spending earns 3 miles/$. SimplyGo uses accumulated SGD charges.
Trust Freedom
Miles mode: 1.3 miles/$ on eligible spending, in S$5 blocks, without a monthly cap or foreign transaction fee. A S$27.25 miles-transfer fee is outside these comparisons.
Payment costs
Amaze linked-card SGD fee: 1%, minimum S$0.50. Foreign purchases: up to 2.1% FX spread. Direct HSBC, Citi and UOB foreign payments incur bank FX charges that are not calculated here. Transfer fees, merchant surcharges and fuel discounts can change the value of a choice.
