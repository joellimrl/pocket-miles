A clear recommendation.

Without an amount: all your wallet cards are shown in descending miles-per-dollar order, with Amaze + Citi included when enabled. This applies to SGD and foreign purchases. It assumes any remaining bonus allowance covers the purchase. Rounding and partially used caps can change the order.

With an amount: cards are ordered by estimated total miles after modelled rounding and bonus allowances. Fees are shown separately and are not deducted from miles; this is not a net-value comparison. Equal miles favour no added fee, then the disclosed Amaze spread over an unmodelled bank FX charge. Cards with excluded rewards or disabled miles mode remain visible with zero miles. Routes we cannot estimate appear last and are marked accordingly. Amaze shares Citi’s bonus allowance. Its foreign spread is up to 2.1%, while SGD payments have a 1% route fee, minimum S$0.50. Transfer fees, merchant surcharges, petrol discounts and promotional targets are outside the ranking.

Saved merchants: your saved category and exceptions are used for each applicable payment method. Online-only merchants show only online; stores can show tap, online and insert together. Different checkouts may have different bank classifications. Search suggestions for a new merchant are assumptions, not a lookup of its actual MCC. A confirmed label means you checked the category yourself.

Freshness: bank rules are a dated snapshot and require maintenance. Allowances are manual and do not decrease when you search. Stored merchant details do not automatically refresh bank rules.

Your data: only your saved wallet and saved places are synced to your private backend. One-off checks stay in memory on this screen; they aren’t sent to the backend or added to the saved list. Up to 500 places can be saved; there is no saved-slot limit on one-off checks. After a successful load, your cached wallet also works for one-off checks offline. Saving needs a connection. Disconnecting clears this browser’s keys and cached data. Revoke its server connection with /pocket devices in Telegram. Each paired browser has its own signing key; paired connections have no automatic expiry. It never needs card numbers or bank logins.
