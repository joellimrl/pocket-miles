A clear recommendation.

Without an amount: we compare current earn rates among your selected cards, prioritising routes without an extra payment or FX fee. This assumes any remaining bonus allowance covers the purchase. Rounding and partially used caps can change the answer.

With an amount: we estimate miles after the modelled rounding and allowances. Amaze may appear separately with its extra fee. Transfer fees, merchant surcharges, petrol discounts and promotional targets are outside the ranking.

Saved merchants: your saved category and method take priority. Search suggestions for a new merchant are assumptions, not a lookup of its actual MCC. A confirmed label means you checked the category yourself.

Freshness: bank rules are a dated snapshot and require maintenance. Allowances are manual and do not decrease when you search. Stored merchant details do not automatically refresh bank rules.

Your data: this app stores your wallet and merchant book on its own server. A copy on this device lets you look up saved merchants offline after a successful load. Saving needs a connection. Disconnecting clears this device’s key and cached data. It never needs card numbers or bank logins.
